Hi! I'm Lucie, currently a research intern in Generative Modeling between MAP5 (Université Paris Cité), ISIR (Sorbonne Université UPMC) and LTCI (Institut Mines-Télécom).

My research mainly focuses on generative deep learning models and their applications to inverse problem solving.

Previously I was a reserach Intern at EPFL's ECEO lab under Devis Tuia and Li Mi, studying the impact of multi modal models on image geo-localization tasks.

My personnal interests range from physics all the way to computer programming. I find generative modelling in recent years has artfully blended insights from these different fields which motivates my research.
